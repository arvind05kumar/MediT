1. EXECUTIVE OVERVIEW & VALUE PROPOSITION
================================================================================
MediT is an on-demand pharmaceutical logistics network designed to provide 
instant medicine delivery (10-30 minutes) while strictly enforcing clinical 
safety, doctor prescription validation, drug contraindication detection, and 
massive cost reductions for patients through generic substitutes.

Unlike standard grocery/food delivery apps, pharma delivery requires:
- Reading complex and illegible handwritten doctor prescriptions.
- Verifying drug-drug interactions before checkout to prevent medical accidents.
- Ensuring a registered pharmacist verifies all scheduled drugs (Schedule H).
- Providing affordable Jan Aushadhi generic bioequivalent alternatives.

3. CORE GEMINI AI ENGINES & BACKEND ENDPOINTS
================================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.1. MULTIMODAL PRESCRIPTION OCR (`POST /api/ai/read-prescription`)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Technology: Google Gemini 1.5 Flash Vision Multimodal API.
- Prompt Configuration: `src/config/ai-prompts.ts` -> `PRESCRIPTION_OCR`.
- Payload: `{ imageBase64: string, mimeType: string }`.
- Capabilities: Reads doctor handwriting, identifies medicine names (e.g., Augmentin 625 Duo), 
  strengths (625mg), dosage forms (Tablet), quantities (10 Tabs), dosage instructions 
  ("1 tab twice daily for 5 days"), doctor registration details, and outputs a confidence score (0-100%).
- Fallback: Contextual AI engine activates if no API key is supplied, ensuring demos never fail.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.2. AUTOMATED DRUG INTERACTION CHECKER (`POST /api/ai/check-interactions`)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Technology: Google Gemini 1.5 Flash Clinical Pharmacology System.
- Payload: `{ cartMedicines: string[], userHistory: string[] }`.
- Clinical Rules Checked:
  * Blood Thinners (Ecosprin / Aspirin) + NSAIDs (Combiflam / Ibuprofen) -> High Risk GI Bleed Warning.
  * ARB Antihypertensives (Telmisartan) + Potassium Supplements -> Hyperkalemia Risk.
  * Oral Hypoglycemics (Metformin) + Multiple Anti-diabetic duplications.
- Response Schema: `{ hasInteraction: boolean, warnings: [{ medicines: [], severity: 'High'|'Moderate'|'Low', explanation: '', recommendation: '' }] }`.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.3. JAN AUSHADHI GENERIC SUBSTITUTE FINDER (`POST /api/ai/generic-alternative`)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Technology: Indian Pharmacopoeia & Jan Aushadhi generic mapping model.
- Payload: `{ brandName: string, saltComposition: string, price: number }`.
- Output: Bioequivalent generic name, Jan Aushadhi price, and exact ₹ savings (up to 60-70% discount).
- Example: Augmentin 625 Duo (₹204.50) -> Jan Aushadhi Amoxy-Clav 625 (₹85.00) -> Saves ₹119.50!

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.4. 24/7 AI HEALTH CHATBOT - MEDIBOT (`POST /api/ai/chat`)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- System Instruction: Empathetic clinical pharmacy assistant for Indian users.
- Boundaries:
  * Only gives guidance for minor, OTC symptoms (mild tension headache, acidity, cold, sore throat).
  * Strict prohibition against prescribing Schedule H / controlled drugs.
  * Mandatory disclaimer appended to every answer:
    "⚠️ Disclaimer: This is not a substitute for professional medical advice. Consult a doctor for serious or worsening symptoms."
  * Immediate emergency escalation for chest pain, shortness of breath, or trauma -> calls 112 / 108.

================================================================================
5. STEP-BY-STEP END-TO-END WORKFLOW (THE COMPLETE DEMO JOURNEY)
================================================================================

Step 1: Website Entry & Vitals Check
- User visits http://localhost:3000.
- Full-screen Beating Heart and animated ECG pulse appear, running diagnostic progress.
- Smoothly fades out and reveals the clean white customer app with light apple green tiles.

Step 2: Medicine Search & Voice Assistant
- User searches "Augmentin" or clicks the Mic icon to speak in Hindi/English ("Augmentin dikhao").
- User adds "Augmentin 625 Duo" (Rx) and "Dolo 650" (OTC) to the cart.

Step 3: Prescription Upload & Gemini OCR Extraction
- User opens Cart -> Checkout is locked with a "Prescription Required" alert.
- User clicks "Upload Prescription (AI OCR)".
- User selects sample preset "Dr. Verma (Chest & Fever Rx)".
- Gemini Vision OCR deciphers doctor handwriting, extracting Augmentin 625, Pan-D, Dolo 650 with 94% confidence.
- User clicks "Confirm & Attach to Order".

Step 4: AI Clinical Safeguards & Generic Savings
- Cart automatically runs `/api/ai/check-interactions` against user history.
- Generic substitute suggestion appears: "Save ₹119.50 with Jan Aushadhi generic".
- User clicks "Swap & Save".
- User chooses "Express Emergency" delivery slot and selects UPI payment.
- User clicks "Place Order" -> Confetti celebration triggers and redirects to `/orders`.

Step 5: Human-in-the-Loop Pharmacist Verification
- Switch to Pharmacy Admin (`/pharmacy-admin`).
- Registered Pharmacist reviews the pending order: original prescription image on left, Gemini OCR entities on right.
- Pharmacist inputs verification note and clicks "Approve & Authorize Dispense".

Step 6: Delivery Partner Dispatch & Progress Tracking
- Switch to Delivery Partner Portal (`/delivery`).
- Rider sees the top Emergency order.
- Rider clicks "Picked Up from Pharmacy" -> "Out for Delivery" -> "Mark as Delivered ✓".

Step 7: Real-Time Customer GPS Radar
- Return to Customer `/orders` screen.
- Notice the GPS route radar and 6-stage timeline update in real time without refreshing!

Step 8: 24/7 MediBot Consultation
- User clicks floating "Ask MediBot AI" icon.
- User asks: "I have mild acidity after spicy food, what can I take?".
- MediBot recommends Digene / Pan-40 OTC relief with mandatory clinical disclaimers.


================================================================================
6. HUMAN-IN-THE-LOOP COMPLIANCE & SAFETY SAFEGUARDS
================================================================================
Crucial Differentiator for SIH & Legal Drug Regulatory Compliance:
- AI is strictly a decision-support and OCR extraction accelerator.
- AI NEVER automatically dispenses Schedule H antibiotics or prescription drugs.
- A licensed, registered pharmacist must visually review the prescription and click 
  "Approve" in the Pharmacy Admin portal before any rider is dispatched.

8. DATABASE ARCHITECTURE (SUPABASE SCHEMA)
================================================================================
The project includes `supabase_schema.sql` defining:
1. `users` (id, name, phone, age, addresses jsonb, active_medications, role).
2. `medicines` (id, name, brand_name, salt_composition, price, mrp, requires_prescription, stock_qty, pharmacy_id).
3. `pharmacies` (id, name, license_no, address, lat, lng, contact, rating).
4. `orders` (id, user_id, items jsonb, total_amount, delivery_fee, status, delivery_type, ai_extracted_data, assigned_pharmacy_id, assigned_delivery_partner_id).
5. `prescriptions` (id, order_id, image_url, doctor_name, extracted_medicines jsonb, verification_status).
6. `refill_reminders` (id, user_id, medicine_id, frequency_days, next_reminder_date, active).
7. `chat_history` (id, user_id, messages jsonb).
